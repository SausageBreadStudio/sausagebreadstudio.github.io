DriveZand

A delivery-driving life-sim

Drive a delivery-van across 2.500km²* of hilly woods and open countryside. Try not to starve, dehydrate, or fall asleep at the wheel.

Same as a real job, basically.

almost the size of Luxembourg

  • Open world: 2.500km² of open world with no loading screens
  • Customizable car: Swap out the engine, respray your car, put on a sturdier bumper
  • Survive: Manage your money, hunger and sleep, just like a real delivery driver
  • Stars over the valley
  • Morning on a tree-lined road
  • Dusk on the winding road
  • Headlights on the gravel path
