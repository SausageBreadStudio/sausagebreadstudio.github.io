DriveZand

A delivery-driving life-sim

Drive a post van across 2,500km² of open Brabant countryside — about the size of Luxembourg — delivering packages while keeping yourself fed, watered, and rested.

  • One continuous world the size of a small country, no loading screens
  • A post van you can fully customize, from engine to paint job
  • Your own needs — food, drink, sleep — run alongside the job
  • PC, with mouse or gamepad support

About

Sausage Bread Studio is one person, based in the Netherlands, building DriveZand start to finish. The name is a nod to the Brabant sausage roll: unpretentious, regional, better than it has any right to be.
