DriveZand

A delivery-driving life-sim nobody asked for, but here we are

Drive a post van across 2,500km² of open Brabant countryside, about the size of Luxembourg, if Luxembourg had more mud and worse weather, delivering packages while trying not to starve, dehydrate, or fall asleep at the wheel. Same as a real job, basically.

  • 2,500 km² of open Brabant countryside, about the size of Luxembourg, one continuous world with no loading screens
  • Fully customizable post van, from engine to paint job. Yes, you can put a spoiler on it. No, it will not make it faster
  • Life-sim needs: food, drink, and sleep run alongside the job, just like a real delivery driver, minus the union
  • PC, with mouse or gamepad support

About

It's just me, one guy in Brabant with a laptop, too many ideas, and a mild inability to leave this alone. No publisher, no team, no investors asking about "synergy," just someone who thought "what if the post van game was really, really big." The name's a nod to the local worstenbroodje: nothing fancy, nothing international, but somehow exactly what you wanted.
