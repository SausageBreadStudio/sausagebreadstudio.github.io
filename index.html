DriveZand

A delivery-driving life-sim

Drive a delivery-van across 2,500km² of hilly woods and open countryside (in total about the size of Luxembourg). Deliver the packages while trying not to starve, dehydrate, or fall asleep at the wheel.
Same as a real job, basically.

Releasing on Steam for Windows PC.

  • 2,500 km² of open world with no loading screens
  • Fully customizable car, from engine to paint job to trinkets
  • Life-sim needs, manage your money, hunger and sleep, just like a real delivery driver, minus the union
